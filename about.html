About CHENHUA INTERNATIONAL

Powering Agriculture Since 2018

Founded in 2018, CHENHUA INTERNATIONAL has become a trusted global supplier of Agricultural Machinery Wheels and Agricultural Machinery Tires. With years of expertise and innovation, we have grown from a local brand into an international company, serving farmers, distributors, and industries worldwide.

Our mission is to deliver durable, reliable, and high-performance solutions that meet the toughest demands of modern farming and transportation. We believe in combining engineering precision with customer-focused service to build long-term partnerships.

Business Scope

  • Agricultural Machinery Wheels
  • Agricultural Machinery Tires
  • Custom OEM & Export Solutions
  • Worldwide Distribution Network

Our Values

  • Innovation: Constantly improving to meet market needs.
  • Durability: Products built for strength and longevity.
  • Trust: Global partnerships with honesty and transparency.
